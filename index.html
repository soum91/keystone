Keystone Bridge

Puts your Tuya lights in Apple Home and turns them off when the TV goes off.

Install

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer. iPhone and iPad can't do this at all.
This page has to be opened over https.

Pick the port that appears when you plug the board in (on this board it's USB-SERIAL CH340 or wchusbserial). The right firmware for your chip is picked automatically. Tick Erase device on the first install.

Repair or update, keeping everything

For a bridge that's already set up but stuck or crash-looping. Rewrites only the firmware: Wi-Fi, Apple Home and app pairings stay. Do not tick Erase device.

No port shows up?

  1. Cable. Most micro-USB cables in drawers are charge-only. If nothing appears when you plug in, try another cable first.
  2. Windows: install the CH340 driver from WCH (CH341SER.EXE), unplug and replug. Device Manager should show USB-SERIAL CH340 (COMx).
  3. Mac: recent macOS has the driver built in. If nothing shows, install WCH's macOS driver and allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  4. Stuck on "Connecting"? Hold the BOOT button (next to the USB port), tap EN/RST, let go of BOOT, then try again.
  5. Close anything else using the port (Arduino IDE serial monitor, another flasher tab).

After it installs

  1. The screen on the board says Wi-Fi setup. Either click Connect above again and choose Connect to Wi-Fi (over USB), or on your phone join the open network Keystone-Setup: the setup page pops up. Pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. The Apple Home code is 466-37-726 unless you set your own.
  2. The board's screen shows its IP. In Keystone (or Afterglow: Settings › Apple Home bridge) it finds and pairs it.
  3. Home app › + › Add Accessory › More options › Keystone Bridge.
